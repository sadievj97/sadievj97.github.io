Intercept Versus Slope: Evidence from One Career

Jakhongir Sadiev

Harvard, master's in economicsNew York, NYOctober 2026

Abstract

Hiring usually ranks candidates by their intercept: years in the exact role. I argue the slope matters more. Across one career spanning Central Asia, East Africa, and the Americas, I document repeated entry into new sectors followed by fast delivery. The candidate's steep learning curve overtakes his lower starting point within about three months.1
